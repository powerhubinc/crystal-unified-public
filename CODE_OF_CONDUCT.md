# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and professional environment for
everyone who contributes to or interacts with Crystal Unified.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional
- Provide constructive feedback
- Focus on technical merit
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Respect differing viewpoints

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling or inflammatory comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Spam or off-topic content

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:
- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Project documentation
- Official project communications

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to: conduct@powerhub.inc

Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly. Responses may include:
- Warning
- Temporary ban
- Permanent ban

All reports will be kept confidential.
